A Round That Changed the Conversation

Round 10 delivered the kind of result that shifts the entire narrative. Holmes and Gardens posted 1,381 — an absolutely outrageous score that obliterated Van Twists by 347 points. That's not a win. That's a statement of dominance. Matthew's side extend their lead at the top of the PPR table to 11.36 and look utterly untouchable.

The other major story: DC & Tax Payers surge to 2nd on the PPR table. Rupe's side didn't play this round but their consistency across the season sees them leapfrog Broncic in the rankings — moving from 4th to 2nd at 11.10. Meanwhile, Broncic had their worst round in weeks, going down 1,019 to 1,036 against a resurgent Foopys. A one-point loss in AFL fantasy terms — the cruelest kind. William's mob drop to 3rd.

Dark Magicians continue their strong form with 1,300 over Shavas, staying 5th. DC & Tax Payers edge past Flagdons in the table, with Flagdons slipping to 4th despite a decent enough score losing narrowly to DC. Phillipou bounce back with 1,231 over IZaks — a strong return that nudges them up the table. And Foopys — yes, Foopys — finally pick up a win. The wooden spoon race just got interesting.
