How we got here

From 7th to the cup

Nobody had the Twists down for anything in 2026. They were 4 and 7 after Round 11, they spent the year dragging the Max Hall waiver controversy behind them and they squeezed into the finals in 7th.

Then the run started. Higher seeds fell one after another, and on Grand Final day they took down Better Holmes and Gardens, a side that had sat on top of the Power Rankings for almost the entire season. The favourite fell, the minority shareholder won, and the league will never hear the end of it.

    Unfinished business: the Grand Final

    William

    Walked into 2026 as the reigning champions and had to watch somebody else lift the cup. Getting it back is the only acceptable outcome at Broncic HQ.

    Unfinished business: the crown

  • Flagdons

    Rick

    Climbed to 3rd on the Power Rankings by Round 9, then slid two spots in two weeks. The poster says it all. Rick needs a full season, not half of one.

    Unfinished business: the back half

  • Foopys

    Miller

    Topped the Draft Night rankings, then spent the winter on Wooden Spoon Watch, familiar ground for the 2024 spooners who did the pub chase in the rain. Back-to-back wins to close out Round 11 suggest there is still life in this squad.

    Unfinished business: living up to the draft

  • Phillipou

    Cam

    Patrick
