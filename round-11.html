The Back Half Begins in Earnest

Eleven rounds in and this competition has crystallised. Holmes and Gardens stay #1 at 11.44 PPR — winning again, this time over DC & Tax Payers 1,159 to 1,005. The margin of their season-long leadership is undeniable. Two clear at the top, and no sign of the machine stopping.

The big story this round: Broncic get back to winning ways — 1,150 over Dark Magicians 1,111 — and reclaim 2nd place on the PPR table. William's response to the Round 10 Foopys humiliation was emphatic. Meanwhile, Dark Magicians slip to 3rd despite posting 1,111, leapfrogged by the resurgent Broncic. Their position in the top three remains strong, but the table is tightening.

The lower half has its own chaos. Foopys beat Phillipou — yes, again — with Miller's side proving Round 10 was no fluke. 1,109 to 981 is a comprehensive performance. Phillipou drop to 11th on the PPR table after back-to-back losses and what had looked like a revival is now looking shaky. Nethers men also get a big win over Flagdons — 1,138 to 973 — halting Flagdons' top-four ambitions. The table after Round 11 is the clearest picture yet of where each team actually stands.
